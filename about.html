About the firm

Enterprise-grade controls, with a partner's attention.

SDDA is a firm of Chartered Accountants established in 2022, with offices in Mumbai and Pune.

Who we are

Partners in finance, compliance and automation

Our partners bring more than a decade of experience in internal audit, risk consulting and financial controls, gained at KPMG, EY and British Petroleum. That includes reviews across India, Vietnam, Russia and Malaysia, and leading multi-location, multi-process reviews end to end.

We started the firm on a simple conviction: every business, whatever its size, deserves enterprise-grade controls, decision-ready financial reporting and smooth compliance. It should come with the personal attention, agility and care of a partner who understands the business.

Our team brings together Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, MBAs and Excel & automation specialists. Every engagement is handled by people who understand both the numbers and the business behind them.

Our vision

To help founders and leadership teams build and scale with confidence, as their trusted partner for finance, compliance and automation, so they can focus on what they do best.

Our mission

To bring clarity, control and efficiency to every business we serve, combining professional expertise with smart automation for accurate, timely, decision-ready support that grows with our clients.

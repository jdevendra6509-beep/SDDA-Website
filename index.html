Who we are

Enterprise-grade controls, with a partner's attention

SDDA is a firm of Chartered Accountants established in 2022, with offices in Mumbai and Pune. Our partners have spent more than a decade in internal audit, risk consulting and financial controls at KPMG, EY and British Petroleum. They have led multi-location, multi-process reviews across India, Vietnam, Russia and Malaysia.

We started the firm on a simple belief: every business, whatever its size, deserves strong controls, decision-ready reporting and smooth compliance. Our team of Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, MBAs and Excel & automation specialists understands both the numbers and the business behind them.

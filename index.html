Who we are

Enterprise-grade controls, with a partner's attention

SDDA is a firm of Chartered Accountants established in 2022, with offices in Mumbai and Pune. Our partners have more than a decade of experience in internal audit, risk consulting and financial controls at KPMG, EY and British Petroleum, including reviews across India, Vietnam, Russia and Malaysia.

We believe every business, whatever its size, deserves strong controls, decision-ready financial reporting and smooth compliance. Our team of Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, MBAs and Excel & automation specialists understands both the numbers and the business behind them.

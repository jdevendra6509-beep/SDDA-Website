Why it matters

Traditional audit checks a few transactions. Most risks never surface.

A sample is a small window

Errors rarely sit in the typical transaction, and the typical transaction is all a sample can tell you about.

Findings arrive too late

A report issued weeks after quarter-end describes money that has already left. By then the vendor is paid and the credit is time-barred.

Costly software is not the answer

Licences, implementation and maintenance often cost more than the leakage they are bought to find. We use technology you don't have to buy.

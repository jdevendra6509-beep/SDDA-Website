Scope · Concepts 01 & 02

Nine process areas, one control framework

We pick and price each process separately. Start where your exposure is highest, then extend. We share the sub-process checklists before we begin, so you can add your own concerns.

Order to Cash (OTC)

Credit limit breaches, unapproved discounts and credit notes, revenue cut-off, price master vs invoice variance, ageing.

Procure to Pay (PTP)

Duplicate invoices and vendor codes, three-way match failures, bank-detail changes, split POs below approval limits.

Record to Report (RTR)

Entries posted out of hours or by unauthorised users, round-sum and back-dated journals.

Hire to Retire (HTR)

Ghost employees, duplicate bank accounts and PAN, full-and-final settlement delays, attendance-to-payroll variance.

Fixed assets

Capitalisation vs revenue expense, CWIP ageing, checks on depreciation rates and dates, physical verification variance.

System & access controls

User access vs role, segregation-of-duties conflicts, privileged and dormant IDs, generic logins, audit trail settings, change management.

Compliance: GST, TDS, payroll

GSTR-2B vs books ITC matching, blocked credit, RCM, TDS section and rate checks, PF / ESIC / PT / LWF checks.

Treasury: investments & borrowings

Interest recomputation vs sanction terms, covenant monitoring, unused limits, maturity tracking, forex exposure.

Inventory management

Negative stock, slow and non-moving ageing, cycle-count variance, valuation vs NRV, scrap and shortage trends.

How the data testing works

Exceptions, not opinions

Fixed risk checklist

Each process has a ready library of risks and the control that should cover each one. It is built from years of review work across industries.

Your data, in full

We take every transaction from your ERP, not a sample. Tally, SAP, Oracle, Navision, Busy or anything else that produces an extract.

Checks run by tool

Every check runs on every transaction: duplicate payments, threshold splitting, master-data changes and ageing breaches.

Exception report

You get the exception list with the transactions attached, ranked by value at risk, with a recommended control fix for each.

Concept 01 adds vouching

Beyond the data checks, we trace 30 samples per quarter per area back to the underlying documents.

Concept 02 stops at the data

The same checklist and the same 100% coverage, with exceptions evidenced from system data alone.

Common to both

A monthly or quarterly refresh of exceptions, and closure of earlier findings tracked to the end.

Concept 03 · Risk Control Matrix

Tested and scored each quarter

  • Understand the process: walkthroughs with process owners to see how it really runs, which is not always how the manual says it runs
  • Draft the controls: each risk is written into the Excel RCM with its control, owner, frequency and evidence source
  • Identify the gaps: risks with no control, or with a control that cannot be evidenced, are flagged as design gaps
  • Mitigate: we draft a practical control for each gap, sized to your team
  • Test and score: we test samples for each control every quarter and score each control pass, partial or fail

Sample sizes follow how often each control runs and the applicable guidance: up to 5 or 10 documents per control each quarter.

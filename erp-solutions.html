Modules

Pick what you need. We build it end to end.

Start with one module and add more as you grow. Each one is designed with the controls an auditor would look for.

Procure to Pay

Purchase requests, POs, GRN, three-way match, approval limits, vendor onboarding with KYC and bank-change checks by a second person (maker–checker).

Order to Cash

Customer onboarding, credit limits, price masters, sales orders, invoicing, collections and receivables ageing.

Inventory management

Stock in, out and between locations. Reorder alerts, slow-moving stock, valuation and ageing reports.

Fixed asset manager

Asset tagging and one live register. Location, custodian and transfers. Depreciation under both the Companies Act and the Income Tax Act.

Payroll inputs & HR

Attendance-to-payroll checks, employee master controls, duplicate PAN and bank checks, full-and-final tracking.

Compliance tracker

GST, TDS, ROC and labour law due dates with owners, reminders and proof of filing in one place.

Approvals & workflows

Rule-based approvals by value, department and role. No more approvals on WhatsApp or email.

MIS & dashboards

Live P&L, cash, receivables and spend views, with drill-down to the transaction.

Exception monitor

Duplicate invoices, split POs, negative stock and MSME 45-day breaches, flagged as they happen.

Built in, not bolted on

Every build includes

These features come standard with every build. They are what make a system audit-ready.

Role-based access

Users see and do only what their role allows. Segregation of duties is enforced.

Audit trail

Who changed what, when, and the value before and after. It cannot be switched off.

Maker–checker

Master data, bank details and high-value entries need a second person to approve.

Excel export

Every list and report exports to Excel. Your data is always yours.

Any browser

Desktop, tablet or phone, with nothing to install on each machine.

Works with your books

Imports and exports for Tally, Zoho, SAP, Busy and others. Keep your accounting software.

How we build it

From walkthrough to go-live

Discover

We walk through your process with the people who run it and note every rule, exception and sign-off.

Design

Screens, approval rules, roles and reports, agreed with you in writing before we build.

Build

We build in short cycles and you review working screens as we go, not just documents.

Parallel run

The system runs alongside your current process for one full cycle and is reconciled before go-live.

Hand over & support

User training, SOP and walkthrough videos, followed by ongoing support and enhancements.

Automation · Analytics · Training

A process that runs on memory is a process at risk.

Automation does not replace the person or the control. It removes the copying, reformatting and recomputing, and makes the work repeatable. We study a process once, build it into a tool, and hand it to your team to run.

Name one process See examples
Why automate

Four signs a process is ready

It is manual

The same files are opened, copied and reformatted every cycle. Only the effort repeats.

It takes too long

Days of the close go into putting numbers together, which leaves little time to review them.

It invites error

One stale reference or missed row travels quietly into the reported figure.

It rests on one person

When people leave, the know-how leaves too. Once automated, the process runs without specialist skills.

The control stops depending on someone remembering to perform it.
